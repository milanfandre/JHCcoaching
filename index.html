A USA Triathlon Level 3 coach.

Jennifer holds a USA Triathlon Level III certification, the highest coaching credential the organization awards, and is one of only 43 coaches in the country to hold this distinction. She has been coaching endurance athletes since 1999.

Over the past 25 years, she has worked with athletes at every level, from first-time racers taking on new challenges to World Championship podium finishers, Kona finishers, and a bronze medalist at the Rio 2016 Olympic & Paralympic Games. As a lifelong athlete who continues to train and compete herself, Jennifer brings not only decades of coaching expertise but also firsthand experience and perspective to every athlete she works with.
