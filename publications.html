Publications & press

Over the years, Jennifer's coaching has been recognized by leaders in the endurance community and featured by respected organizations within the sport. These stories are not marketing messages, they are reflections from others about her coaching approach, her commitment to athletes, and the impact of the work they have done together.

USA Triathlon Coaching Spotlight

USA Triathlon selected Jen for its Coaching Spotlight, a feature the national governing body publishes to highlight coaches who set the standard for the sport. The piece walks through how she plans a season, how she communicates with athletes, and what has kept her coaching full time since 1999.

The recognition carries weight because it is independent. Jen holds a USA Triathlon Level 3 certification, the highest the organization awards, and the Spotlight is the governing body itself pointing to her work as an example for other coaches.
