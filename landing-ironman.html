Coaching built for the full distance.

An Ironman is a long day, and the training that gets you there has to fit the rest of your life. Jen starts by learning where you are now, what your week actually looks like, and what you want out of race day. Then she builds a plan around all of it.

You get structure across the swim, bike, and run, a fueling and pacing plan you have practiced long before race morning, and a coach who adjusts when work, travel, or a rough week gets in the way. That mix of feel and science is why athletes stay with her for years.

Ironman coaching questions.

How long does it take to train for an Ironman?
Most athletes build over several months. Jen sets the timeline to your starting fitness and your race date, and adjusts as life happens.
Can you coach a first-time Ironman athlete?
Yes. Jen has taken many athletes from their very first triathlon to an Ironman finish line, including complete beginners.
Do you coach remotely?
Yes. Jen coaches athletes worldwide through TrainingPeaks, and works with athletes in the Chicagoland area in person.
What is included in Ironman coaching?
A periodized 140.6 plan, swim, bike, and run structure, race-day fueling and pacing, taper planning, regular feedback, and access to the JHC community and camps.
